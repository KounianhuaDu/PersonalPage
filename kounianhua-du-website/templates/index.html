About

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at Shanghai Jiao Tong University and a member of the APEX Lab. I am advised by Professor Weinan Zhang and Professor Muhan Zhang.

My current research at Tencent Youtu Lab explores cache-enhanced on-policy distillation (CacheOPD), structure-aware multi-agent skill evolution (MultiEvoSkills), and reinforcement learning for MCP-native agents (MCPDisCo). I also study inference-time personalization, code intelligence, recommendation systems, and graph representation learning.

Selected Research

CacheOPD

In Progress

Cache-Enhanced On-Policy Distillation

Explores teacher-cache-guided rollouts to increase beneficial token hits, information-preserving token importance, and distribution-aware curriculum training and regularization.

MultiEvoSkills

In Submission

Structure-Aware Multi-Agent Evolution for Generalizable Skill Artifacts

Uses a structure-aware multi-agent evolution pipeline and distributed workloads to reduce the budget required to produce strong, generalizable skill artifacts through collective intelligence.

MCPDisCo

In Submission

Seesaw-Mitigated Tuning for MCP-Native Agents

Combines token-masked disentangled rewards and gradient surgery to mitigate interference between reasoning and tool use, with evaluation on long-horizon tasks and out-of-distribution generalization.
